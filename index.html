SAMPLE QUESTIONS
Medicine · Cardiology
Which biomarker is most specific for myocardial injury?
Answer: Cardiac troponin.
Troponin I and T are highly specific markers of myocardial injury and are central to the diagnosis of acute myocardial infarction.
Pharmacology
Which drug is a short-acting β₂ agonist commonly used for rapid relief of bronchospasm?
Answer: Salbutamol (albuterol).
It produces rapid bronchodilation and is commonly used as a rescue inhaler.
Pediatrics
Deficiency of which vitamin is classically associated with nutritional rickets?
Answer: Vitamin D.
Vitamin D deficiency reduces calcium/phosphate availability and impairs normal bone mineralization.
